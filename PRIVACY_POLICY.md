# Privacy Policy for Wavefy

**Effective Date:** July 27, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 27, 2026  

Welcome to **Wavefy** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience when using our mobile application (the "App").

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use Wavefy. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the App.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we collect include:

#### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Preferences & Settings:** Your chosen app settings, custom playlists, saved library items, and playback history stored locally on your device or linked to your user profile.
- **Support & Feedback:** Any information you provide when contacting customer support or submitting feedback (e.g., your email address, message content).

#### B. Automatically Collected Data
- **Device & Technical Information:** Hardware model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and network connection details.
- **App Usage Data:** Information on how you interact with Wavefy, such as episodes played, search queries, app crash reports, and performance metrics.

#### C. Android Device Permissions Requested
Wavefy requests certain permissions to deliver its core functionalities:
- **Microphone / Audio (`RECORD_AUDIO`):** Used only if you access voice-activated search or audio features within the app. Audio recorded is processed strictly for the intended feature and is never recorded or stored silently in the background.
- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used to send alerts about new podcast episodes, updates, or playback controls. You can opt out at any time in device settings.
- **Foreground Service (`FOREGROUND_SERVICE`, `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK`):** Allows continuous audio playback when the app is running in the background or when your screen is locked.
- **Storage & System Access (`RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`):** Enables the app to restore background audio queues or notification services after restarting your device.

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:
- To provide, operate, and maintain the Wavefy app and its audio streaming services.
- To organize your library, subscriptions, and custom playlists.
- To send push notifications regarding new podcast episodes and features.
- To analyze app performance, diagnose crashes, and improve user experience.
- To comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

---

### 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Services

We do **not** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized or aggregated data with reliable third-party service providers solely to assist in operating our app:

- **Third-Party Content Providers & RSS Feeds:** When you listen to a podcast episode, your device connects directly to the public RSS feed or podcast media server host to stream the audio file.
- **Analytics & Crash Reporting:** Third-party services (such as Firebase Analytics or Expo services) may collect standard non-identifiable diagnostics to help fix bugs and improve stability.

---

### 4. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion

- Most user data (such as offline downloads, playback progress, and app preferences) is stored locally on your device.
- You can clear your data at any time by clearing the app cache/data from your Android settings or by uninstalling the App.
- If you wish to request the deletion of any data associated with your account/support requests, please contact us at **support@wavefy.app**.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

Wavefy is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will delete it immediately.
